About me

My interest in intelligent systems became concrete during my bachelor’s thesis, where I built a Persian Sign Language recognition system end to end. I collected a custom dataset of 4,070 images from 36 participants, used MediaPipe to detect and crop hands, and trained image-classification models across 37 PSL classes. ResNet152 achieved the best result at 96.4% accuracy.

During my master’s at Sharif University of Technology, I moved into reliability-aware deep learning. My thesis developed a BiGRU-based virtual sensor for electric-vehicle battery temperature estimation, using two-stage training, fault injection and physics-informed constraints to improve robustness under sensor faults.

Alongside academia, I worked on text, image and video processing projects for QCRI. Today I develop software with Python at Steel Alborz, bringing research-oriented thinking into practical software development.
