01 · M.Sc. Thesis

Fault-Tolerant Virtual Sensor for EV Battery Systems

Sharif University of Technology · January 2026

Designed a robust deep-learning virtual sensor to estimate lithium-ion battery temperature and support Battery Management System reliability when physical sensors become inaccurate or fail. The approach uses a BiGRU model, two-stage training and fault-aware evaluation for transient and permanent sensor failures.

02 · B.Sc. Thesis

Persian Sign Language Recognition

Computer Engineering · Computer Vision

Developed a deep-learning based Persian Sign Language recognition system built on a custom dataset that I collected and prepared myself. The project covered the full workflow from photographing gestures and preparing the data to hand detection, preprocessing, model training, and evaluation for Persian sign classification.

4,070cropped hand images
37Persian sign classes
96.4%best test accuracy

What I owned

  • Collected images from 36 participants and built a custom PSL dataset across varied backgrounds and capture conditions
  • Used MediaPipe for hand detection and cropping, then resized images to 224 × 224 for model training
  • Trained and compared ResNet18, ViT, and ResNet152 models in Python and PyTorch
  • Built the recognition pipeline as groundwork for future sign-to-text and sign-to-speech applications
